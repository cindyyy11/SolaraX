# Launch

## Event summary

The MAIC Nexus Challenge launch took place on 11 June 2026 at the Multi Purpose Hall, UOB Plaza 1, Kuala Lumpur.

## What the launch positioned the challenge as

- A national stage for AI builders
- A competition, conference, and incubation pipeline
- A platform to turn young talent's AI ideas into reality and move them toward commercialisation
