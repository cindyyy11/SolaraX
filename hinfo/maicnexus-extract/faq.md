# FAQ

## Eligibility

### Who can apply?

Anyone, any nationality, any age. Each team must include at least one Malaysian citizen, meaning a MyKad holder. Permanent Residents and international students do not qualify as Malaysian citizens. The team leader does not need to be Malaysian. Malaysian citizens living or working abroad are eligible.

### Is there an age limit?

No age restriction. Students, professionals, entrepreneurs, and independent builders all apply on equal terms.

### Do I need to be working full-time on this?

No. There is no academic or professional status requirement. Side projects, university work, employer-permitted projects, and full-time startups are all welcome.

## Teams

### How big can my team be?

One to five members. Solo applications are welcome.

### Can I be on more than one team?

No. One person, one team. Listed on two teams, and both teams may be disqualified.

### Can I add or remove a team member after applying?

Only with written organiser approval, and only before the next stage begins. Mid-stage changes are not permitted.

## Industry and artifacts

### Can I switch industries after submitting?

No. Your industry selection locks at submission and cannot be changed. Choose deliberately.

### What language must materials and presentations be in?

English only. All written materials, pitch deck, project summary, demo video, and artifact documentation, must be in English. All spoken presentations and Q and A sessions during the Semi-Final and Grand Final are conducted in English.

### What artifacts do I need to submit?

A pitch deck in PDF form, a written project summary, and an AI usage disclosure are mandatory at application. A demo video URL, artifact or repo URL, product or technical architecture PDF, and LinkedIn profiles are optional and can be added or updated later from the team dashboard. Technical architecture PDFs must be 5 MB or under.

### Can my repo be private?

No. Artifacts must be publicly accessible without a login wall during judging windows. You may keep it private outside those windows.

### Does making my repo public expose my idea? How do I protect my IP?

Your IP stays yours. Going public for judging does not transfer ownership. The organisers only receive a non-exclusive licence for marketing. To limit exposure, keep the repo private outside judging windows, or submit a hosted demo, API endpoint, or HuggingFace Space instead of full source. A LICENSE file and copyright notice help clarify reuse terms. If the work may be patentable, file before the judging window or consult a Malaysian IP agent.

## Prizes and equity

### How does the equity investment work?

The top three placement awards are cash plus equity packages, subject to the final R and R and award agreements. Champion receives RM200,000 cash plus equity, including RM100,000 equity investment from the organisers. 1st Runner-up receives RM100,000 cash plus equity. 2nd Runner-up receives RM50,000 cash plus equity.

### What if my team isn't incorporated?

All three winning teams must register a Malaysian Sdn. Bhd. with at least one Malaysian director. Incorporation is a condition precedent to receiving any cash or equity. If a team is not incorporated at award time, it has 90 days from notification to register. The Champion has a further 60 days to complete equity onboarding.

### What do finalists who don't win get?

All participants receive a merit certificate. All finalists receive a Huntier talent pipeline listing. The top three teams receive trophies and AI Forum plus Expo access on Grand Final day.

## Cost and logistics

### Is there an application fee?

Yes. Registration is RM150 per team for early bird until 15 July 2026, RM250 per team for fair early bird from 16 July to 8 August 2026, or RM500 per team for standard pricing from 9 August 2026. The fee is paid once per team.

### Do I need to travel to Kuala Lumpur?

Preliminary judging is fully online. Semi-Final in October and Grand Final, estimated for November 2026, are in-person in Kuala Lumpur. Teams cover their own travel unless otherwise announced.

### What about the AI Bootcamp?

It is free, online, runs in June 2026, and is open to all applicants. It includes live sessions and recorded materials.
