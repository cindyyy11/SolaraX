# Application

## What the public page shows

The application flow starts with a six-step process. The public page currently exposes Step 01 — Eligibility.

## Step 01 — Eligibility

### A three-question screener

Confirm three things before the application opens. All three must be Yes to continue.

1. At least one team member is a Malaysian citizen.

- A Malaysian citizen is defined as a holder of a Malaysian Identity Card, MyKad.
- Permanent Residents, expatriates, and international students are not considered Malaysian citizens.

2. My team will be 1 to 5 members in total.

- Solo applications are welcome.
- Maximum five members per team.

3. I have read, or will read, the Terms and Conditions.

- The full T and Cs are on the terms page.
- Submission constitutes acceptance.
