# MAIC Nexus Challenge Overview

## What it is

The Malaysia AI Conference and Challenge is a government-endorsed, open-track AI competition for students, professionals, entrepreneurs, and independent builders.

## Core objective

Build breakthrough applications and infrastructure across six industries that matter to Malaysia's future.

## Competition format

- 300 teams enter
- 100 teams advance
- 10 teams reach the live final
- 1 champion wins

### Stage 1: Preliminary

- Jul to Sep 2026
- Online material review
- 300 to 100 teams
- Prompt: Is this a real attempt at solving a real problem with a real artifact?

### Stage 2: Semi-Final

- Oct 2026
- Live demo day
- 100 to 10 teams
- Prompt: Is this team actually building something that works and can scale?

### Stage 3: Grand Final

- Nov 2026
- Live grand final plus AI conference
- 10 to champion
- Prompt: Is this team investable and is the product ready to commercialise?

## Hackathon-style prompts from the site

- Ship breakthrough applications
- Build infrastructure across the clean energy value chain, healthcare workflows, finance, manufacturing, public services, and ESG/SDG outcomes
- Produce a real artifact that can be reviewed by judges

## Prize summary

- Champion: RM200,000 cash plus equity
- 1st Runner-up: RM100,000 cash plus equity
- 2nd Runner-up: RM50,000 cash plus equity
- Special awards: 5 category awards, cash only, RM5,000 each
- All participants: merit certificate
- All finalists: Huntier talent pipeline listing
- Top 3 winners: trophy and AI Forum plus Expo access on Grand Final day

## Key dates

- 11 June 2026: Official launch event, registration opens
- June 2026: Applications open and free AI bootcamp begins online
- First week of Sep 2026: Preliminary judging
- End Sep 2026: Preliminary results
- Oct 2026: Semi-final demo day in Kuala Lumpur
- Nov 2026: Grand Final in Kuala Lumpur
