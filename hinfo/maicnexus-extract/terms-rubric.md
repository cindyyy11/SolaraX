# Terms and Marking Rubric

## Official position

The public FAQ says that if anything conflicts with the Rules and Regulations document, the R and R wins.

## Definitions

- MAIC: The Malaysia AI Conference and Challenge
- Organisers: Solara Global Media Sdn. Bhd. and Solarvest Holdings Berhad, jointly
- Participant or Team: Any individual or group of up to five members submitting an application
- Team Leader: The team member designated to submit and manage the application on behalf of the team
- Malaysian Citizen Requirement: Every team must include at least one Malaysian citizen, meaning a MyKad holder
- Artifact: A working, judge-accessible deliverable as defined under each Track Axis
- R and R: The official Rules and Regulations document, version 2.1, May 2026

## Eligibility

- Open to applicants of any nationality, age, or professional status
- No academic, employment, or residency requirement
- Each team must include at least one Malaysian citizen, designated by full legal name at application time
- Permanent Residents and international students do not satisfy the Malaysian citizen requirement
- Malaysian citizens living or working abroad are eligible
- Employees and immediate family members of organisers and judges are not eligible

## Team composition

- Team size: one to five members
- Solo applicants are permitted
- A person may be listed on only one team
- Cross-team participation can disqualify all affected teams
- Team additions, removals, or substitutions are only allowed between stages and only with prior written approval
- Mid-stage changes are not permitted

## Registration and format

- Applications open 11 June 2026 at the Official Launch Event
- Applications close 31 August 2026 at 23:59 MYT
- No extensions unless the organisers decide otherwise in writing
- Entry is limited to 300 teams, first come first served
- Competition stages: Application, Online Qualifier, University Semi-Final, Grand Final
- Advancement is determined solely by judging outcomes
- Two-tier agreement model: basic participation terms at registration, stage-two agreement for Semi-Final teams covering confidentiality, intellectual property, data-sharing, and investment-related terms

## Submission and artifacts

- Teams must submit the materials defined on the Apply page by the published deadline
- Pitch deck, project summary, and AI disclosure statement are mandatory at application
- Demo video, artifact link, and member profiles are recommended and may be updated from the dashboard until the relevant judging window
- Artifact links must be publicly accessible during the judging window
- Private repositories, gated demos, or content behind authentication walls count as non-submission
- Repositories used as artifacts must show at least three commits over at least two calendar days, dated before the submission deadline

## Industry selection

- Each team selects one industry from T1 to T6 at submission
- The selection is locked at submission and cannot be changed later
- Material divergence from the declared industry may be disqualified

## Marking scheme

Each criterion is scored on a 1 to 10 scale and weighted to a 100-point total.

### Criteria and weights

- Technical Feasibility: 25%
- Commercial Viability: 25%
- Industry Relevance: 20%
- Scalability: 15%
- ESG or National Impact: 15%

## Prize disbursement

- Total prize pool exceeds RM370,000 in cash and equity investment
- Cash and equity allocation for each category is published before Finals
- Equity is governed by a separate investment agreement
- Trophies, certificates, and merit gifts are provided to relevant award holders
- Cash prizes are paid to the designated Malaysian citizen team member's verified bank account on behalf of the team within 30 calendar days of the Grand Final, subject to incorporation requirements

## Equity investment

- Top three teams, Champion, 1st Runner-up, and 2nd Runner-up, are conditional on incorporating a Malaysian private limited company, Sdn. Bhd., with at least one Malaysian director
- Incorporation is a condition precedent to receiving cash or investment
- If not incorporated at award time, the team must register within 90 calendar days of award notification
- Equity onboarding must be completed within a further 60 days
- Failure to complete equity onboarding forfeits the equity component only, not the cash award

## Investment rights

- Winning teams that accept investment may be subject to time-limited co-investment rights, including a right of first offer and a right of first refusal
- These are benchmark-and-matching mechanisms only
- Teams are free to decline investment or seek funding elsewhere
- Full terms are in the stage-two participation agreement

## Intellectual property

- All IP in submitted artifacts and materials remains with the team
- Organisers acquire no ownership rights in the artifact or underlying IP
- Teams grant a non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide licence for organisers to use submitted materials for marketing, editorial, and convening purposes related to MAIC, with attribution where reasonable

## Disqualification

- Material misrepresentation in the application or anchor designation
- Plagiarism, fraudulent artifacts, or backdated commits used to fake development
- Cross-team participation by any individual
- Track misrepresentation at any stage
- Failure to honour judging access requirements for the artifact
- Conduct that breaches the event code of conduct at any in-person stage

## Privacy and data

- Personal data is processed under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 and the MAIC Privacy Notice
- Data is shared with organisers, judges, and partner organisations only for competition administration and prize disbursement
- Participant data is retained for 24 months after the competition
- Finalist data is retained for 36 months for follow-up purposes
- Data is then deleted or anonymised
- Consent withdrawal requests can be sent to admin@maicnexus.com and are resolved within 14 days subject to legal retention duties

## Amendments

- Organisers may amend the Terms and Conditions and published R and R before applications close
- Material amendments will be published and emailed to applicants
- Continued participation after notification means acceptance

## Governing law

- Governing law is Malaysia
- Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia
